**Title:** Seva Jump
**Short description:** A free skyward adventure inspired by Sikhi. Four modes, two jumpers, boosts, and badges!

**A skyward seva adventure — now updated to version 1.0.11.**

The birds have flown away with the parshad! Choose a young Sikh boy or girl, bounce through a bright Gurdwara-inspired world, and help bring it back. Your jumper bounces automatically; you guide each leap.

**Also on mobile:** [Google Play for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.sevajump.game) · [App Store for iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/seva-jump/id6822047487).

The browser game and offline ZIP remain free below. Progress is stored locally and does not sync between browser and app versions.

### Play four ways

- **Endless Run:** keep climbing and beat your personal best.
- **Arcade Mode:** reach 1,000 points and break through the finish banner. Birds arrive gradually as you climb.
- **Challenge Mode:** collect all 50 parshad bowls and reach the finish. A missed-bowl warning helps you spot an incomplete run.
- **Hard Mode:** tackle smaller moving and breakable platforms, with birds arriving earlier.

### Collect, upgrade, and explore

- Collect **parshad bowls** for score and **Khanda tokens** for upgrades earned through play.
- Bounce across grass, spring, moving, and wooden breakable platforms.
- Catch a **Kara boost** for a higher jump or a **Nishan boost** for a short flight that you can steer.
- Unlock **Falcon Save** to catch a fall, **Dhal Shield** to block a bird collision, and permanent **Power Jump** levels.
- Choose which owned upgrades to use before each run. Choices are remembered; unused Falcons and shields are kept. Each run can activate one Falcon and one shield.
- Earn **14 badges** and follow your personal bests and statistics in **Records**.
- Use the optional **Helping Hand** in Endless and Arcade when a broken route leaves the next platform out of reach.

A first-run guide, adjustable music and sound, reduced motion, and About & Help make it easy to get started. Designed for ages 8–15; everyone is welcome.

### Controls

**Touch:** drag left or right across the game. Lift and reposition your finger when needed.
**Mouse:** drag to steer.
**Keyboard:** A/D or Left/Right Arrow keys to steer; Escape to pause. Enter or Space starts Endless from Home when a button is not focused.
**Standard controller:** left stick or D-pad to steer; A to start, confirm upgrade choices, or resume; B or Start to pause or cancel choices.

Use the pause button or Escape, then Resume to continue. Fullscreen gives the portrait play area more room. Menus can scroll on shorter screens.

### Free to play, with progress on your device

No ads, purchases, accounts, or analytics in the game. Scores, upgrades, badges, statistics, and settings stay in this browser on this site; they do not sync between devices. Clearing browser or site data may erase them. If saving is blocked, you can still play for the current session. The itch.io website has its own privacy policy.

**Play offline:** download the free version 1.0.11 ZIP below, extract it, and open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation or game account is needed.

### Inspired by Sikhi, made for everyone

*Seva* means service with love and humility, without expecting a reward. Here, parshad means karah parshad, a blessed sweet offering shared equally with everyone. A Gurdwara is a Sikh place of worship, learning, and community gathering, open to everyone. This is one creator’s make-believe adventure inspired by Sikhi; it invites optional curiosity and does not replace Sikh teachings. About & Help explains the terms and offers an optional learning link.

Created by **Khalsa Game Studio**. The Sikh creator directed and approved the cultural content. Artwork and synthesized music were made with ChatGPT assistance from the creator’s ideas; no outside assets were used. Code and text were also developed with AI assistance.

3. Check the ZIP SHA-256 against its sidecars. Extract to a fresh folder; test the exact extracted files over HTTP and from local `index.html`.
4. Keep the previous approved ZIP. Do not upload native builds, tooling, or the repository itself.
5. Test the exact ZIP and media on a private/restricted itch draft before switching the public upload. Check controls, pause, Settings, fullscreen, saves/reload, links, and hosted offline behavior where possible; distinguish unverified checks.
