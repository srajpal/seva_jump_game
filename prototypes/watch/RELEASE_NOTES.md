# Watch prototype history

Local simulator experiments only; none is published or included in the iPhone/iPad release.

## 0.7.0 / build 7 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Dragging follows the finger directly, with no steering catch-up delay.
- After the intro, mixes wide grass, narrow grass and crumbling wooden platforms.
- Parshad appears on roughly one in three platforms instead of every landing.
- Retains Crown steering, revised spacing, music and local high scores.

## 0.6.0 / build 6 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Stabilized gameplay timing against delayed timer callbacks during input.
- Caps recovery after a long stall and discards paused time on Resume.
- Keeps the revised spacing, music and separate local high score.

## 0.5.0 / build 5 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Replaced the abrupt hard spacing with a gradual ramp: 40-unit gaps/28–40 side shifts, then 42-unit gaps/36–52 side shifts.
- Keeps Endless, small breakable platforms, music, local high score, birds and one rescue.
- Added accessible next-platform position and actual music playback status.
- Simulator-only; iPhone/iPad app remains unchanged.

## 0.4.0 / build 4 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Added an original looping gameplay track, with a separate saved Music toggle and preview.
- Music pauses with the run, stops when leaving, and resumes only when enabled. Audio controls are also available from Pause.
- Hard-stage vertical gaps increase to 44 units; side shifts increase to 48–72 units for more active Crown/drag steering.
- Retains a forgiving five-platform intro, breakable platforms, high score, birds and one rescue.

## 0.3.0 / build 3 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Endless-only with a saved high score and new-record result.
- Five forgiving solid platforms, then small platforms that crumble on landing.
- Quiet landing, crumble, rescue and finish effects; separate sound/haptic controls and sound preview.
- Retains occasional birds and one automatic rescue.

## 0.2.0 / build 2 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Added a short 150-point Arcade mode, bird obstacles and one automatic rescue.
- Retained Endless and isolated local best score. Arcade was subsequently removed based on owner feedback.

## 0.1.0 / build 1 — prepared October 13, 2026

- Initial standalone Watch prototype: Endless, Crown/drag controls, pause/resume and local best.
- Reused existing artwork independently; validated on 42mm/46mm simulators.
