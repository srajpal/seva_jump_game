# Badge progression

September 27, 2026. Four new badges and the Boost Seeker rename are included in 1.0.8 / Android build 46, installed locally on Pixel 6. The creator approved Both Feet In and Comeback Kid; Clean Flight was rejected. Order is an approximate easiest-to-hardest progression, not a claim about every player's experience.

| Order | Badge | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First Leap | Finish a run. |
| 2 | Sky Starter | Reach 100 points. |
| 3 | Boost Seeker | Collect 5 Kara/Nishan boosts total. |
| 4 | Bowl Collector | Collect 50 parshad bowls total. |
| 5 | Khanda Keeper | Collect 10 Khanda tokens total. |
| 6 | Bird Defender | Block 3 bird collisions total. |
| 7 | Boost Master | Collect 25 Kara/Nishan boosts total. |
| 8 | Banner Breaker | Complete Arcade. |
| 9 | Endless Explorer | Reach 1,000 in Endless. |
| 10 | Comeback Kid | Complete Arcade after using a Falcon Save in that run. |
| 11 | Both Feet In | Complete Arcade with each character, across runs. |
| 12 | Challenge Champion | Collect all 50 bowls and complete Challenge. |
| 13 | Sky Legend | Reach 2,000 in Endless. |
| 14 | Perfect Arcade | Complete Arcade with every parshad bowl on the playable course. |

Boost Seeker retains the `power-seeker` save ID. Historical boost totals count toward both boost badges. Bought upgrades are not collectible boosts. Perfect Arcade tracks bowls removed below the screen as missed and checks remaining bowls at completion; objects above the final banner are excluded when the runway is fixed. It requires at least one collected bowl and never awards on a loss. Both Feet In tracks future wins by character because older saves did not record that detail. Perfect Arcade and Comeback Kid cannot be inferred from historical aggregate statistics.

Medals reuse the existing art family: Perfect Arcade has the banner medal with a gold star, Boost Master the boost medal with two purple stars, Both Feet In the first-leap medal with a paired-direction mark, and Comeback Kid uses the falcon artwork. Badges are listed in reading order, left to right, then down. Scrolling remains available when the expanded collection cannot fit a small screen.
