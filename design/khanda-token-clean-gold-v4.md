## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one alternative production Khanda collectible token for the children's game Seva Jump, transparent PNG square. Reference 1 is the current token: preserve recognizable Sikh Khanda component arrangement (central double-edged sword, circular chakkar behind, symmetric curved kirpans), but redesign the surrounding coin. Reference 2 shield and reference 3 avatars establish the game's bright illustrated cel-shaded style. New direction: simple warm golden coin rim, deep navy-blue inset face, large clear ivory/silver Khanda occupying most of the inner face. Remove all four diamonds, cyan outer ring and busy decorative trim. Front-facing circular coin with just a small lower-right thickness edge, broad two/three-tone gold shading, one restrained upper-left highlight. Crisp dark outlines, clean smooth illustrated edges matching avatar rather than jagged faux-pixel texture. Symbol must have ample negative space and remain clear at only 38 pixels wide; avoid tiny detail, excessive beveling or thin gaps. Coin occupies 90% canvas, centered, fully visible. Truly transparent background. No glow, aura, sparkles, cast shadow, text, people or Guru imagery. One single token only.
