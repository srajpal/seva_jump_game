About Seva Jump

Game guide

Seva Jump is a playful game inspired by Sikh community values. These short notes introduce the terms used in the game.

Items and challenges

  • Parshad bowl: adds to your score.
  • Khanda token: currency for the upgrade shop.
  • Platforms: grass, spring, moving, and one-use wooden planks.
  • Kara boost: one higher jump.
  • Nishan boost: one stronger jump and bird protection for 5 seconds.
  • Falcon Save / Dhal Shield: protect one fall / one bird hit.
  • Birds: avoid them unless you are protected.

Sikh terms

Seva
Selfless service and helping others.
Parshad
A blessed sweet food shared with the congregation.
Gurdwara
A Sikh place of worship and community gathering.
Kara
A steel bracelet, one of the Sikh articles of faith.
Nishan Sahib
The Sikh flag, seen outside gurdwaras.
Khanda
A widely recognized Sikh emblem.
Dhal
A shield; in the game, it blocks one bird collision.
