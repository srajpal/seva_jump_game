About Seva Jump

☁
Your mission

Birds have scattered the parshad. Leap through the sky, collect it, and help bring it back.

A playful jumping adventure inspired by Sikhi and its values of equality, kindness, and seva. Everyone is welcome to play.

✦ Collect, jump, protect

Parshad bowlAdds to your score.
Khanda tokenSpend these on upgrades.
PlatformsGrass is steady; spring launches, moving shifts, and wood breaks.
Kara boostGives one higher jump.
Nishan boostA short guided flight you steer, plus 5 seconds of bird protection.
Falcon SaveCatches one fall during a run.
Dhal ShieldBlocks one bird collision.
🕊BirdsAvoid them unless you are protected.
!Hard ModeOnly small moving and breakable routes. Birds arrive earlier, one per screen.

↔ How to play

Phone or tablet

Drag anywhere across the game to guide your jumper left and right.

Desktop

Drag with a mouse or steer with A/D or the arrow keys. Enter or Space starts Endless from Home; focused buttons keep their usual action. Escape pauses. Use ⛶ during play for fullscreen.

Controller

Use a standard gamepad's left stick or D-pad to steer. A starts Endless from Home, confirms the pre-run upgrade choices, or resumes a paused run. B or Start pauses, or goes back from the upgrade choices.

◌

Reduced Motion keeps gameplay the same while making decorative movement still, including character bobbing, hovering, fireworks, and transitions.

✦ Learn about Sikhi

Sikh
A learner and disciple of the Guru who follows Sikhi.
Sikhi
The Sikh path of learning and living by the teachings of the Gurus, beginning with Guru Nanak Sahib.
Seva
Serving others with love and humility, without expecting a reward.
Parshad
Here, karah parshad: a blessed sweet offering shared equally with everyone, Sikh or non-Sikh.
Gurdwara
A Sikh place of worship, learning, and community gathering, open to everyone.
Kara
A steel bracelet, one of the Sikh articles of faith.
Nishan Sahib
The Sikh flag, seen outside Gurdwaras.
Khanda
A widely recognized Sikh emblem.
Dhal
A shield; in the game, it blocks one bird collision.

A note from the creator

I am a follower of Sikhi, and this game is my creative idea, inspired by Sikhi and made for everyone. Familiar symbols become playful parts of a make-believe adventure. I hope they spark questions, not tell you what to believe. The game does not replace Sikh teachings or learning from others.

I welcome corrections to facts and feedback about anything that gives the wrong impression. People may see the creative choices differently, and we can disagree respectfully.

Curious about Sikhi?

Wondering about a kara, a Gurdwara, or seva? Explore the Sikh Research Institute (SikhRI), or visit a local Gurdwara and ask about its teachings and visitor customs. Younger players can explore with a parent or trusted adult.

Privacy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Seva Jump (SevaJump) is a free, offline-friendly game from Khalsa Game Studio, listed on Google Play as Khalsa Game Studio Games. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your scores, settings, upgrades, badges, and statistics are stored only on your device to remember your progress. You can erase them with Reset all progress in Settings.

Browser saves belong to this browser and site. Clearing site data can erase them. If saving is unavailable, you can still play for the current session.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you reset it or the app or browser storage is cleared. On Android, you can also clear the app's storage in device Settings. We do not receive a copy and cannot recover deleted progress. There are no accounts or cloud saves.

Playing on another website

The game itself has no tracking. A website hosting the game, such as itch.io, may process information under its own privacy policy.

What we do not use

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising or purchases
  • No analytics or tracking cookies
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

Children

The game does not collect personal information from children or adults.

Privacy questions and support

Email khalsagamestudio.apps@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests for information you have sent us. Your email address and message are processed through our email provider to respond and may remain until deleted. You can request deletion, subject to legal retention obligations. This is separate from gameplay; the game sends no automatic diagnostic or gameplay reports.

Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

Policy updates

Changes will be shown with a revised date and reflected in applicable store disclosures. External websites and email services operate under their own privacy policies.
