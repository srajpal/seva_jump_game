About Seva Jump

☁
Your mission

Birds have scattered the parshad. Leap through the sky, collect it, and help bring it back.

A playful jumping adventure inspired by Sikh community values. Here is everything you need for your next run.

✦ Collect, jump, protect

Parshad bowlAdds to your score.
Khanda tokenSpend these in Upgrades.
PlatformsGrass is steady; spring launches, moving shifts, and wood breaks.
Kara boostGives one higher jump.
Nishan boostOne stronger jump plus 5 seconds of bird protection.
Falcon SaveCatches one fall during a run.
Dhal ShieldBlocks one bird collision.
🕊BirdsAvoid them unless you are protected.

↔ How to play

Phone or tablet

Drag anywhere across the game to guide your jumper left and right.

Desktop

Drag with a mouse, use ← and →, or use the on-screen arrow buttons. Press Escape to pause.

◌

Reduced Motion keeps gameplay the same while making decorative movement still, including character bobbing, hovering, fireworks, and transitions.

✦ Sikh terms

Seva
Selfless service and helping others.
Parshad
A blessed sweet food shared with the congregation.
Gurdwara
A Sikh place of worship and community gathering.
Kara
A steel bracelet, one of the Sikh articles of faith.
Nishan Sahib
The Sikh flag, seen outside gurdwaras.
Khanda
A widely recognized Sikh emblem.
Dhal
A shield; in the game, it blocks one bird collision.
