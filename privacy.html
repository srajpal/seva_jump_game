Privacy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Seva Jump is a free, offline-friendly game. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your scores, settings, upgrades, badges, and statistics are stored locally on the device where you play. They are used only to remember your progress and can be erased with Reset all progress in Settings.

What we do not use

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking cookies
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

Children

The game does not collect personal information from children or adults.
