Seva Jump

Privacy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Seva Jump (SevaJump) is a free game from Khalsa Game Studio, listed on Google Play as Khalsa Game Studio Games. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Khalsa Game Studio

What stays on your device

Your scores, settings, upgrades, badges, and statistics are stored locally on the device where you play. The game uses them only to remember your progress. You can erase them with Reset all progress in Settings.

Browser saves belong to this browser and site. Clearing site data can erase them. If saving is unavailable, you can still play for the current session.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you reset it or the app or browser storage is cleared. On Android, you can also clear the app's storage in device Settings. We do not receive a copy of your saved progress and cannot recover it after deletion. The game has no account or cloud save.

Playing on another website

The game itself has no tracking. A website that hosts the game, such as itch.io, may process information under its own privacy policy. Optional links to Khalsa Game Studio and learning resources open external websites with their own privacy policies.

What we do not use

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking cookies
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

Children

The game does not collect personal information from children or adults.

Privacy questions and support

Contact khalsagamestudio.apps@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests for information you have sent us. If you email us, your email address and anything you choose to send are processed through our email provider to respond to your request. This is separate from gameplay. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information; children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

Support correspondence may remain in our email account until deleted. You can request its deletion at the address above; any information that must be retained to meet legal obligations is an exception. The game does not automatically send diagnostic reports or gameplay data with your message.

Changes to this policy

Updates will appear here with a revised date. Any future change to the game's data practices will be reflected in this policy and applicable store disclosures.
